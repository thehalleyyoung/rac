# Scoring controls and 0–4 anchors

`scoring_controls.py` defines five concrete anchors for each existing content
dimension. Task-specific success criteria remain in the case and accompany the
rubric in blind requests. Retail usefulness includes a supported next step,
activation/invalidation or a justified wait condition. More tools, longer prose
and unauthorized actions do not establish usefulness.

`audit_controls` consumes a predeclared positive/negative control roster, a
two-family judge roster and bound judgments. It retains missing, foreign,
duplicate, wrongly versioned and out-of-range results as failures and compares
each dimension against declared intervals. A judge that awards every answer
four or every answer zero fails the paired synthetic controls. Use this audit
after blind response binding and the existing answer-record validator; this
function alone does not authenticate an answer, a family or a model call.

Six controls reach those positive/negative boundaries and preserve compatibility
with finite fractional scores in the old wire format. Five category anchors do
not establish equal intervals or sufficient resolution. The control audit
introduces no weights, scalar ranking, critical-flag verdict or claim that a
synthetic expected label validates a financial judge. Freeze rubric/control
expectations before model calls; retain individual family results/disagreement.
Actual judges, task applicability and a later human audit remain open. Rollback
stops consuming this additive gate without changing historical scores.
